1,000 Villages.
One Device per Village.

Our call: 1,000 diagnostic platforms — each comprising a tablet and an ultrasound probe — for 1,000 villages in the Global South.

A concrete goal for a global movement

devices.
1,000 villages.

One tablet, one ultrasound probe and modular diagnostic software. This is an international call and outreach initiative: an ambition, not a funded or committed delivery programme.

What it is about

More than distribution. Connection.

The device is a tangible starting point for relationships, practical experience and an international community of researchers and developers.

01 / MAKE IT VISIBLE

Communicate

A tangible demand shows what Modular MedTech stands for and whom the movement aims to reach.

02 / CONNECT LOCALLY

Connect

Bring together medical facilities, researchers, developers and local initiatives.

03 / LEARN IN THE FIELD

Learn

Gain real-world experience, feedback and insight into needs previously overlooked.

04 / BUILD TOGETHER

Co-develop

International partners contribute as developers of independent modules and applications.

Our ambition
We do not merely take technology into the world. We want to mobilize the world’s knowledge to develop new technology.
Global co-creation

Local partners are more than users.

They know medical needs, resources and real workflows from direct experience. Their feedback should generate new research questions and components.

The open architecture makes it possible to reuse locally developed capabilities around the world.
