The central thesis

Capabilities instead of
complete products.

Conventional medical software bundles many functions into complete process chains for specific use cases. Even testing a single idea becomes costly.

We develop small, semantically described modules that work independently and can be assembled into different applications. This lowers barriers for researchers and developers worldwide.

The first research project

One functional end-to-end proof.

We aim to show that independent components can be assembled into a complete diagnostic application.

The Device

One tablet.
Many diagnoses.

A multimodal Android platform uses camera, audio and touchscreen. An ultrasound probe is the first external extension. Two distinct diagnostic tasks, multiple user roles and replaceable modules demonstrate the architecture.
